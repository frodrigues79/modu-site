Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

MODU is operated by HOUSE 05, Unipessoal Lda, Porto, Portugal (the data controller). For any question about your data, email modu@house05.eu.

What we collect

Only what you send us through the forms on this site: your name, email, company or brand, website, and the information you add about your production or capabilities. If you book a call, Calendly processes your name, email and the time you choose.

Why we use it

  • Strategy Call form: to prepare the call, reply to you and send a proposal if you ask for one (steps taken at your request before a contract).
  • Supplier form: to keep a registry of manufacturers and contact you when a client brief fits your capabilities (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored in Google Sheets (Google) and notified by email. Bookings are handled by Calendly. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which also provides privacy-friendly visit statistics without cookies. Videos from YouTube load only when you click to play them.

How long we keep it

Strategy Call details are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless we start working together. Supplier registry entries are kept until you ask us to remove them.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use or withdraw consent, by emailing modu@house05.eu. You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD, cnpd.pt).

Cookies

This site does not use cookies of its own. Calendly and YouTube may set cookies when you open the booking calendar or play a video.
