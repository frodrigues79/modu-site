Questions

Common questions.

How is MODU different from a sourcing agent?

A sourcing agent is usually paid by factories or on order volume, so the incentive is to close the deal. MODU is paid by the brand only, with no commissions from factories. Our incentive is to protect your production, and we stay through sampling, production and reorders, not just the introduction.

Do I need to be in Portugal during production?

No. That is the point of MODU. We are your presence on the ground, so you do not need to be here for every stage. Many founders visit once to meet their factories, and MODU handles the rest locally.

What happens if something goes wrong mid-production?

The work is built to catch problems before they become crises: a structured pre-production check and a weekly Go / Watch / Flag update. If something escalates, you hear it from us first, with what caused it and what is being done.

Do you work with home textiles and leather goods, not just apparel?

Yes. MODU covers apparel, home textiles and leather goods. The process is the same. The factory network is different.

Are factory visits included?

Visits and travel are quoted and invoiced separately for each engagement, so you only pay for the visits your project needs.

What happens on the Strategy Call?

It is a free 30-minute call about your product, stage and timeline. It ends with a clear next step: a written proposal, or an honest answer that MODU is not the right fit.
